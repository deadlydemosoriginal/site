I began my time at Deadly Demos starting out as Isaac’s producer for his radio show, before we swiftly decided to make the show a collaborative effort, then along came the weekly genre changes, and most importantly the tattoos.

Deadly Demos for me is an archive of 3 lads from 3 corners of the country, with the same passion for music trying to find a way to express our interests and our friendship. As we went through University we realised that clubbing wasn’t for us, so Saturday nights were now radio nights, with a lot of laughs and great music blasting through the airwaves.

I am now a graduate of Film and TV studies and an ex Head of Music of University Radio Nottingham, and as me and Isaac settle into graduate life, it’s time for Deadly Demos to trancend being just a radio show and evolve into a multimedia platform, getting involved with Reels, Articles, Album Reviews, Playlists etc., you name it, we’ve got it!

If you would like to get in touch, I am an aspiring multimedia journalist, with massive interests in all kinds of music, so don’t be afraid to shoot me a message, I am always open to new opportunities!
