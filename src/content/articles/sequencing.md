A record has a shape. It starts somewhere, it goes somewhere, and — if the
person sequencing it knew what they were doing — the order in which those
things happen is doing about a third of the work.

## Side one, track three

There is a reason the third track was traditionally the best one. You have
the listener's attention by then, and they have not yet got up to do
something else. Everybody who made records for a living knew this.

Streaming does not have a track three. It has a front-loaded first
thirty seconds, repeated indefinitely, and a tail nobody reaches.

## What we do about it

Play the whole thing, in order, on the radio. It is not a sophisticated
intervention but it is the only one available to us.
